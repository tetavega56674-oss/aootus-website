Product details

Key components matched as one system

These T05 / T06 system components are matched by track profile, curtain type, mounting method, and project layout. Confirm the required parts together for a complete wholesale, project, or OEM quotation.

T05 / T06 Track Profiles

White aluminum track profiles for standard and ripplefold curtain systems. Profile selection is matched with curtain weight, mounting method, and project span.

Runner Options

Independent square-eye runners, connected ripplefold runners, and round-eye runners are available to match different curtain tapes and pleat systems.

End Cap Options

Matched end caps close the profile, retain the runners, and provide a clean finished end for standard or ripplefold track assemblies.

Ceiling Mounting Clip

The metal top clip locks into the upper track channel for direct ceiling installation. Clip quantity is matched with track length, load, and ceiling condition.

Wall Mounting Bracket

The L-shaped wall bracket supports track installation above windows or openings where direct ceiling fixing is not suitable.

Straight Track Connector

The internal metal connector joins two straight track sections, keeps the running channel aligned, and supports longer project spans.

90-Degree Corner Connector

The prefabricated elbow joins straight track sections at a right angle for L-shaped layouts, corner windows, and room divider routes.

Master Carrier

The lead carrier controls the curtain leading edge and supports opening and closing operation in standard or ripplefold curtain systems.

Curtain Tape

Standard or ripplefold curtain tape connects the fabric to the runners and helps form the required pleat spacing and curtain presentation.

Standard curtain Ripplefold curtain

Standard and Ripplefold Curtain Options

Standard Curtain

Uses separate runners without a connecting cord. Each runner moves independently and can be spaced to suit the curtain width and pleat requirement.

Components: aluminum track, end cap, independent runners, master carrier, straight connector, 90-degree elbow connector, mounting clips, and standard curtain tape.

Ripplefold Curtain

Uses runners connected by a spacing cord at fixed intervals. The carrier set works with ripplefold tape to maintain regular, evenly spaced folds.

Components: aluminum track, ripplefold end cap, connected ripplefold runners, master carrier, straight connector, 90-degree elbow connector, mounting clips, and ripplefold curtain tape.

Custom Curved Layouts

Factory machine-bent curved tracks can be customized from project drawings or templates for bulk engineering projects and retail businesses that require ready-shaped curved track orders. Hand-bendable track profiles are also available as a separate option for manual on-site adjustment.

  • Factory machine bending for engineering batches and retailer custom orders
  • Hand-bendable track for flexible on-site adjustment
  • Straight, L-shape, U-shape, bay window, and custom routes
