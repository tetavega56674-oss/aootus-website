System position

Direct ceiling installation prepared as one supply package

This system is selected when the track should sit close to the ceiling with a clean, low-profile appearance. Track, fixing clips, running parts, connectors, and curtain components are matched before quotation.

1

Flush Installation

Top clips lock into the track and fix it directly against a suitable ceiling surface.

3

Product details

Ceiling mount components matched as one system

T05 / T06 Track Profiles

White aluminum profiles for direct ceiling installation, selected by curtain weight, span, shape, and finish requirement.

Single Ceiling Clip

The metal clip locks into the upper track channel and fixes a single rail directly to the ceiling.

Ceiling Fixing Package

Track, clips, screws, runners, end caps, and connectors are prepared together for installation.

Runner Options

Independent runners, breakaway runner strips, and fixed-spacing ripplefold runners can be selected.

End Cap and End Stop Options

Matched end parts retain the runners and provide a clean finish at each track end.

Straight Connector

The internal connector aligns two track sections for longer ceiling-mounted runs.

Corner Connector

A prefabricated elbow creates right-angle routes for corner windows and divider layouts.

Master Carrier

The lead carrier controls the curtain edge and supports smooth opening and closing.

Curtain Tape

Standard or fixed-spacing tape matches the selected runner and required curtain presentation.

Installation planning

Ceiling fixing and project layout options

Confirm the ceiling base, track layout, fixing interval, curtain weight, and access for installation before production and packing.

Direct Ceiling Installation

Single or parallel rails can be fixed directly to concrete, timber, or prepared ceiling structures with matched clips and fasteners.

  • Confirm ceiling material and fixing point strength
  • Match clip spacing to track span and curtain weight
  • Prepare access for screws, connectors, and end stops

Track Layout and Curving

Factory machine-bent track can be supplied from drawings for engineering batches and retailer custom orders. Hand-bendable profiles are available for selected on-site layouts.

  • Straight, L-shape, U-shape, arc, and bay window routes
  • Factory bending from project drawings or templates
  • Standard and ripplefold curtain movement planning
