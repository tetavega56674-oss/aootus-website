Product details

Key rod components matched as one system

Component selection is based on rod profile, opening width, curtain heading, mounting surface, support interval, finish, and packing requirement.

Standard Rod Profile

Aluminum round rod profile with an integrated lower runner channel for smooth curtain movement.

Integrated Runner Channel

The concealed lower channel supports gliders while maintaining the visual appearance of a curtain rod.

Runner and Glider Options

Matched plastic runners support standard hooks, rings, or curtain heading accessories.

Wall Mounting Bracket

Single or double brackets hold the rod at the required projection from the wall and window frame.

Center Support

Additional support controls rod deflection across long openings or heavier curtain applications.

Finial and End Cap

End parts close the rod, retain the runner set, and provide a finished appearance in matched colors.

Rod Connector

Internal connectors join rod sections for long spans while keeping the profile and runner channel aligned.

Finish Options

Black, white, anodized silver, and project-specific finishes can be discussed by quantity.

Complete Hardware Set

Rod, runners, brackets, supports, finials, connectors, screws, anchors, and instructions can be packed together.

Installation planning

Rod configuration and project mounting options

For project quotation, confirm the opening width, return allowance, wall or ceiling condition, curtain layers, curtain weight, bracket projection, and support quantity.

Single and Double Rod Options

A single rod supports one curtain layer. Double brackets can combine sheer and blackout curtains with suitable wall projection.

  • Single rod for one curtain layer
  • Double rod for sheer and blackout curtains
  • Bracket projection matched to curtain clearance

Span and Support Planning

Long openings require suitable rod strength, bracket spacing, and center support positions based on curtain weight and wall condition.

  • Confirm wall material and anchor type
  • Add center supports for long or heavy spans
  • Prepare equal bracket positions for repeated rooms
