Two hospital product styles

Choose Different Product Styles

Both options belong to the Hospital Curtain Track System family. Choose the product style first, then confirm direct ceiling mount or suspension rod mount during quotation.

Style 1

Classic Fixed Hospital Curtain Track System

A classic fixed divider curtain track system for hospital wards, clinics, treatment rooms, and commercial privacy areas. It supports both direct ceiling installation and suspension rod installation.

  • Fixed curtain divider route for stable project layouts
  • Supports top mounting clips or suspension rod support
  • Matched with runners, hooks, rods, connectors, end caps, and accessory sets
Open Style 1 Detail

Style 2

New Sliding Divider Curtain Track System

A newer sliding divider curtain system. When not in use, the divider curtain can slide and park near the wall; when needed, it can slide out to divide space flexibly. It also supports direct ceiling installation and suspension rod installation.

  • Sliding collection near the wall when the divider is not needed
  • Flexible space division for wards, clinics, and multi-use rooms
  • Matched with track, suspension parts, runners, hooks, connectors, and accessory sets

What to confirm after choosing the style
